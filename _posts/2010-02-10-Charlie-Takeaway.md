> to proceed(verb) vs proceeds (plural noun)
Sunt doua cuvinte diferite chiar daca proceeds provine din verbul proceed.
to proceed = a continua, a merge mai departe
Please, proceed with the plan.
He proceed to explain the situation
proceeds = incasari, venituri obisnuite, suma rezultata dintr-o tranzactie
The proceeds from the sale will be donated to charity
The proceeds of the auction were $10k.
The company sold the building for $5 million. The proceeds $5 million, but the profit was only $1 million.
